## Owner clarification during V6

The owner confirms two distinct entrance layers: building entrances in Academic
facades **and** boundary gates in the campus wall. Of the six boundary gates:
main/rear oppose one another; two are at the ends of roads extending right/left
from Academic; two relate to the upper right/left parking areas. The association
G4/G6 with Academic road termini and G3/G5 with upper parking is an **estimated
map correlation**, not surveyed coordinates or official gate numbering. Existing
G1–G6 IDs/coordinates/spawns remain stable. P12/P14 letters do not authenticate
any perimeter gate. No further owner confirmation of dormitory block identity
or internal gate exact coordinates has been supplied.
